The COMPR flow

Four steps. One considered decision.

COMPR stays out of the way until you choose to run a price check.

  1. 01

    Open a product page

    COMPR accepts a specific product page—not search, category, social-media or map pages.

  2. 02

    Select “Find This Cheaper”

    The check starts only after your conscious click or tap.

  3. 03

    Review an honest outcome

    See a sufficiently reliable match, or a clear no-match message when confidence is too low.

  4. 04

    Continue to the retailer

    You decide whether to open an offer. Confirm price and availability with that retailer.

Why COMPR

Accuracy matters more than filling the screen.

“No reliable match” is more useful than the wrong size, model or variant.

Match

Product and variant signals

COMPR checks key identifiers and characteristics such as brand, model and variant details when available.

Control

Nothing starts automatically

Opening a page is not a price check. External searching begins only when you ask for it.

Restraint

A no-match is valid

Timeouts, provider errors, cached responses and no-match outcomes do not count as successful free checks.

Choice

The retailer completes the sale

COMPR does not sell products. You purchase from the external retailer under its terms.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does COMPR compare?

Specific products and important variant details visible on product pages. Search, category, social-media and map pages are not accepted.

Does COMPR always find the cheapest offer?

No. Coverage and retailer data vary. COMPR only shows offers that pass its matching checks; verify final price and availability.

When does COMPR access a product page?

It reads the active page when you open the extension. External searching starts only after you select “Find This Cheaper”.

Does COMPR sell the product?

No. COMPR is a comparison aid. Any purchase is made from the external retailer.

Is Safari available?

Not yet. Safari support is in development and has not completed conversion, signing, TestFlight or physical-device testing.

How can I get support?

Use the support page to report a problem or contact the beta operator.

How is my data handled?

The website has no analytics or ad trackers. See the Privacy Policy for extension data.
